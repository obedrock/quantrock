💰 Bet Sizing Controls how much money is wagered per detected goal
Bet Size Per Goal
How much to wager each time a goal is detected and a market is found. Contracts are calculated automatically based on the market price.
dollars per goal
Maximum Bet Cap
Hard ceiling on any single bet. Even if the Kelly calculation suggests more, this cap is never exceeded. Protects against runaway sizing on cheap contracts.
dollars maximum
Minimum Balance Buffer
The bot will stop firing bets if your Kalshi balance drops below this amount. Prevents betting your entire account dry.
dollar buffer
🎯 Market Filters Controls which Kalshi markets the bot will bet on
Max Market Price
Skip any market already priced above this. If a market is at 55¢ after a goal was scored, it's already repriced — no edge left. Lower = stricter filter.
cents maximum
Duplicate Goal Cooldown
After a bet fires for a player, ignore further goal events for that same player for this many seconds. Prevents double-betting the same goal across multiple NHL API polls.
seconds
🛡️ Rate Limits & Safety Prevents over-betting and protects your account
Max Bets Per Hour
Hard limit on how many bets the bot can fire in a rolling 60-minute window. NHL games average 3–6 goals so 10/hr is conservative. Increase if running multiple games.
bets per hour
Auto-Bet Enabled
When ON, bets fire automatically without confirmation. When OFF, the bot detects goals and logs them but does NOT place any orders — useful for testing.
Enabled
